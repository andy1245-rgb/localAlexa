Notes on provenance, so a reader can tell what was read and what was not:

- The Gemma config.json and README are behind manual approval at `google/gemma-3-270m`, so
  `hidden_size` and `max_position_embeddings` were read from two independent conversions of the same
  checkpoint (`onnx-community/gemma-3-270m-it-ONNX` and `mlx-community/gemma-3-270m-bf16`, which
  agree) and cross-checked against Google's own published context figure and parameter split.
- Parameter counts are Hugging Face's `safetensors.total` metadata, not the card's rounded headline,
  except where a card figure is also quoted.
- No source read publishes a CPU latency figure, a per-utterance cost, or a CPU fine-tuning cost for
  any of the ten entries. The Qwen3-Embedding technical report, the SmolLM2/SmolLM3 cards, the Qwen3
  cards and the Gemma model card and blog contain no such table; the only numeric speed-adjacent
  claim in the family is Gemma's phone-SoC battery figure above.
- Contrastive support above means "documented by the model's own authors". The decoder checkpoints
  that lack it are not claimed to be incapable of it — only that nothing published says how.
